# data/raw/

Put **`marketing_campaign.csv`** in this folder. The dataset is not stored in this
repository; download it from its original source.

## How to get it

1. Open https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/imakash3011/customer-personality-analysis
2. Sign in (a free Kaggle account works) and click **Download**.
3. Unzip the download and copy `marketing_campaign.csv` into this folder.

## About the dataset

Customer Personality Analysis - 2,240 customers of a retail company (food, wine and gold
products). One row per customer with:

- **People:** birth year, education, marital status, income, children and teenagers at home,
  enrolment date, days since last purchase, complaints
- **Products:** amount spent in the last 2 years on wine, fruit, meat, fish, sweets and gold
- **Promotion:** purchases with a discount, acceptance of 6 marketing campaigns
- **Place:** purchases through the website, catalogue and store; website visits last month

There is no target label, which makes it suitable for unsupervised learning.
